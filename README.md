# Synthesis of Magnesium-Gallic Acid (Mg-GA) MOF

Experimental wet-lab synthesis and preliminary characterization of a biocompatible Magnesium-based Metal-Organic Framework (MOF) for potential drug-delivery applications.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on the green synthesis of a Metal-Organic Framework using Magnesium chloride and Gallic acid. Because magnesium is highly biocompatible and gallic acid is a natural antioxidant, the resulting Mg-GA MOF is a promising candidate for biomedical applications, specifically targeted drug delivery.

## 🧪 Materials & Equipment
- **Precursors:** Magnesium chloride ($\text{MgCl}_2$), Gallic Acid.
- **Solvents:** Distilled water, Ethanol.
- **Equipment:** Magnetic stirrer (REMI 1MLH), Centrifuge, pH meter, UV-Visible Spectrophotometer.

## 🔬 Synthesis Protocol
The MOF was synthesized via a controlled precipitation method:
1. **Preparation:** Separate aqueous solutions of Gallic acid and $\text{MgCl}_2$ were prepared.
2. **Mixing:** The solutions were combined and subjected to continuous magnetic stirring at ~470-500 rpm.
3. **Precipitation:** The reaction was maintained at a pH of 8.01 until a distinct white precipitate formed.
4. **Purification:** The precipitate was isolated via centrifugation and washed 3-4 times with an ethanol/water mixture to remove unreacted precursors.
5. **Drying:** The purified sample was dried to yield the final white Mg-GA MOF powder (Yield: 0.384 g).

## 📊 Characterization
- **UV-Visible Spectroscopy:** Preliminary optical characterization was conducted to evaluate the structural and chemical properties of the synthesized framework.
